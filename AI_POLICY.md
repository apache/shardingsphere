# AI-Assisted Contribution Policy

AI-assisted contributions are welcome, but the human contributor remains fully
responsible for the submitted work. This policy applies to code, tests,
documentation, designs, images, and other project artifacts.

## Contributor Responsibilities

- Understand, review, and be able to explain every submitted change.
- Verify the change to the same standard as any other contribution. AI use does
  not reduce the requirements in `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` or other project rules.
- Check generated output for correctness, security, compatibility, and
  unintended disclosure before submitting it.

## Disclosure

Disclose material AI assistance in the pull request description. Name the tool
and identify the affected files or scope. Assistance is material when generated
or substantially revised content forms a meaningful part of the contribution.

Routine completion of tokens or boilerplate, search and navigation, formatting,
and spelling or grammar corrections do not require disclosure.

Do not include prompts, conversation transcripts, credentials, or private data
in the disclosure. A `Generated-by:` commit trailer is optional.

## Provenance and Licensing

Contributors must comply with the
[ASF Generative Tooling Guidance](https://www.apache.org/legal/generative-tooling.html).
Ensure that the tool's terms permit the submitted use and that generated output
does not contain incompatible third-party material. Identify and handle any
third-party material under the ASF licensing and third-party works policies.

The project does not maintain an approved list of AI tools. Tool choice does not
transfer responsibility away from the contributor.

## Sensitive Information

Do not send credentials, tokens, private communications, non-public security
reports, personal data, private logs, or other confidential information to an
AI service unless that service and use have been explicitly authorized for the
data involved.

Maintainers review AI-assisted and non-AI-assisted contributions under the same
quality standards. They may ask for additional explanation or verification when
a contribution cannot be reviewed with reasonable confidence.
